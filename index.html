Artist Statement
My practice is rooted in an observation of the natural world.

I am interested in the irregular, the eroded and the constantly changing forms that echo the movement of water and the environments that surround us.

About the artist

I am Madi Sexton, a ceramic artist based in Aotearoa New Zealand.

My work draws from my connection with the ocean and the environments around me, translating organic structures, textures and movement into ceramic forms.

Working primarily with stoneware, I embrace the unpredictability of the material and allow natural processes to influence each piece.
